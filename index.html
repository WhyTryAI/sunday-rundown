    1. Creative Agent now works across Adobe's flagship desktop apps like Illustrator and Premiere, handling multi-step edits while you stay in charge. LINK

  2. Anthropic news:
    1. Claude Fable 5.2 is a faster and cheaper upgrade to Anthropic's most capable public model, with better coding, reasoning, and vision. LINK
    2. Claude Sheets and Claude Whiteboards let paid users build shared spreadsheets and whiteboards from a prompt, then export them to tools like Excel or Miro. LINK
    3. Interactive connectors let you open apps like Canva and Notion right inside Claude and watch it edit your files in real time. LINK
  3. Automattic launched Spacefast, a free-to-start home where your AI assistant can publish the apps and websites it builds and hand you a shareable link. LINK
  4. Edge0 open-sourced Edge0-145B, a coding and reasoning model built to run offline on a high-end laptop through a free desktop app. LINK
  5. Google news:
    1. Flow got five new creative tools designed by artists, including one that turns a single phone shot into multiple camera angles. LINK
    2. Gemini 4 Carbon is Google's new strongest model, now rolling out to AI Ultra subscribers in the Gemini app and AI Mode in Search. LINK
    3. Gemini 4 Hydrogen is a fast everyday model in the Gemini app that also powers a Search upgrade built to handle messy, multi-part questions. LINK
    4. Gemini Intelligence on Pixel 10 phones can now turn your spoken rambling into tidy written text as you talk. LINK
    5. Gemini on Apple CarPlay brings hands-free Gemini Live conversations to iPhone users while they drive. LINK
  6. Meta released Muse Ember, its smallest and cheapest multimodal model, built for quick high-volume jobs like coding subtasks and document analysis. LINK
  7. MiniMax open-sourced H3-Image, a 4K image model that can work from up to 16 reference images and finish a picture in about ten seconds. LINK
  8. Nuance Labs launched Emma, a video AI persona that reacts with facial expressions and times its replies like a person on a video call. (Try it for free.) LINK
  9. OpenAI launched Businesses in ChatGPT in beta, letting US businesses claim their profile and build branded agents that answer customer questions inside ChatGPT. LINK
